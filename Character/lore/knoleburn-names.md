# Knoleburn Names

What each name means in the Alzenhiem naming tradition, and why it was given.

In Alzenhiem, a name is not decoration. It is the first charge laid on a life. Crest and sigil are shaped to serve the name. The name is shaped to serve the hope the family speaks over the cradle.

## Place and house

### Alzenhiem

**Said:** Al zen heim

**Meaning:** sheltered home under a kind sky

**Sense in the old tongue:** *Alzen* for refuge that still lets light in; *hiem* for home that stays.

**Why the hamlet bears it:** The founders wanted a name that promised roof and welcome before it promised greatness. Alzenhiem means you may be small and still be held.

### Knoleburn

**Said:** Knole burn

**Meaning:** stream by the knoll; the family that stays where water and rise meet

**Sense in the old tongue:** *Knole* for the low hill above the lane; *burn* for the brook that keeps the fields alive.

**Why the house bears it:** The Knoleburns are not named for thrones. They are named for a place that feeds people: high enough ground to build, wet enough ground to grow. The surname says belonging to land before belonging to legend.

## The family

### Dolkin (Dol kin)

**Meaning:** steadfast of kin; the firm portion given to the family

**Sense:** *Dol* for a firm allotted strength; *kin* for blood and household duty.

**Why he was named this:** His parents saw a child who would become a pillar, not a banner. Dolkin means the strength in him was never meant to be private. It was meant to be spent on family, work, and keeping others upright.

### Fulfein (Fule fe in)

**Meaning:** full fair light; spirit filled with gentle shining

**Sense:** *Ful* for fullness; *fein* for fair radiance that does not blind.

**Why she was named this:** They saw warmth that could fill a room without demanding it. Fulfein means abundance of heart, and light soft enough that people come closer instead of turning away.

### Dezco

**Meaning:** stands firm; one who holds his ground

**Sense:** a short name with a hard center, given when a child already felt like a stake driven true.

**Why he was named this:** Dolkin and Fulfein wanted a name that could survive hard weather. Dezco means do not drift. Stand. Even before red and black were painted onto his crest, the name asked him to be balance that does not collapse.

### Kalhien (Kal he en)

**Meaning:** rises in pursuit; the one who climbs toward what he seeks

**Sense:** *Kal* for upward drive; *hien* for the chase that refuses to stay behind.

**Why he was named this:** Even as a baby he strained forward. They named him for rising and pursuit so his hunger would have honor of its own. Kalhien means your path is ascent, not imitation.

### Astiale (as T ale)

**Meaning:** gentle star; soft rising of dawn light

**Sense:** *Asti* for star or bright point; *ale* for a mild rising, like breath or morning.

**Why she was named this:** She was born into a house that already knew absence and training yards. They refused to load her with iron. Astiale means shine kindly. Rise without hardening. Be the light that makes home recognizable from far away.

## How name, crest, and sigil fit

| Name | Name meaning | Crest / sigil answer |
|---|---|---|
| Alzenhiem | sheltered home under a kind sky | roof, wheat, lane |
| Knoleburn | stream by the knoll | the house tied to living land |
| Dolkin | steadfast of kin | stone arch and hammer |
| Fulfein | full fair light | leaf and crescent |
| Dezco | stands firm | red and black blade of balance |
| Kalhien | rises in pursuit | forward chevron in blue and copper |
| Astiale | gentle star | soft bud or star in rose and cream |

The crest does not invent the meaning. It paints the meaning the name already spoke.
