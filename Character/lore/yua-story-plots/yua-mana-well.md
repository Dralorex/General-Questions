# The Mana Well Yua Bestowed

*Yua story plot. Dezco did not know this system by name when it first moved through him.*
*Yua meant to stay quiet until he asked, or until he figured it out alone. After Saltrock, waiting looked like cruelty.*

## What she placed in him

When Yua charged Dezco as Hero, she also set a special magic inside him without explaining it.

She created a stored mana pool within the confines of his soul, while keeping that pool separate from the soul itself. It is held in him, shaped around his spirit’s borders, but it is not his soul and cannot be mistaken for it. Think of a well built in the courtyard of a house: the house is still the house; the well is still water you can draw.

Dezco’s private name for it, once he understands, may simply be the Well. Yua may call it whatever older tongue she prefers. The truth is practical:

1. Mana gathers and waits in the Well
2. The Well sits within his soul’s confines
3. The Well remains separate from the soul
4. Drawing from it does not spend his soul, only the stored mana

## What the Well is for

Using the mana pool, Dezco can increase his skill with swords by imbuing them with mana.

A proper imbue can make the blade:

1. Lighter in the hand
2. Sharper at the edge
3. Paired with a short burst of greater swiftness and agility in Dezco himself

The burst is brief. It is not endless god speed. It is a drawn breath of power, then the body and blade return to what training alone can hold.

## The innate simple imbue

When Yua bestowed the Well, she also gave him the innate ability of a simple imbument.

Once he understands it, using that simple imbue should feel as easy as breathing. No long chant. No rare catalyst. Intention, breath, steel, and the Well answering.

Advanced control will not be free. He will need training to:

1. Learn how to correctly use the skills
2. Sharpen the timing of draws from the Well
3. Imbue without wasting mana
4. Move with the burst instead of being thrown by it
5. Keep sword forms honest when the blade suddenly weighs less and hungers more

## Why Yua stayed quiet

Yua wanted to avoid talking about the Well unless Dezco asked, or unless he figured it out on his own.

She has watched mortals break under gifts explained too early, and watched others grow sharper by discovering power with their own hands. So she left the Well unnamed in him: present, usable, unexplained.

That plan lasted until Saltrock.

## First use: Saltrock street battle

During the attack after the protective staff fell (journal entry 03), Dezco used the simple imbue without knowing he had done it.

He swung at a short bow creature he had only read about. Height alone would have been trouble. The Well made it worse and stranger. The innate imbue woke in panic and purpose, then shoved him forward: a sudden dash of about fifteen feet while his sword came across from one side to the front of himself.

He was not prepared for the movement. The world arrived too fast. The blade cut empty air above the short creature. Momentum and surprise put him face first in gravel.

That faceplant was not only bad aiming. It was an untrained Well answering a trained swordsman’s panic.

The creature nearly killed him at point blank range afterward. An elf girl’s arrow saved him. Yua watched the whole ugly lesson.

## After the biff

Yua had wanted discovery, curiosity, a question from him first.

After watching him biff a live battle while fully unprepared for his own gift, she decided waiting was a bad idea. Silence had almost written his ending on Saltrock gravel.

On the tomb return, alone in the woods near Saltrock, she remembered more than she had on the mountain. She named the gift as coming directly from Lord AO. They would have to grow together and learn the power through training. No shortcuts. No temper.

She also warned that she feels a darkness unlike any she has known. Source unknown yet.

## Second use: keep goblin fight

During the troll breach at the keep, Dezco used the simple imbue again, this time with less accident and more hunger.

The blade felt eager. His body matched it. He surged forward, cleaved one goblin at the waist, and the momentum carried the edge to a second goblin more than ten feet away, opening his gut. Still untrained, but no faceplant.

## What remains to teach

She will still explain the Well in full: what it is, that it is not his soul, how simple imbue should feel once understood, and that sword mana can lighten, sharpen, and briefly hasten him. Naming Lord AO is not the same as mastery. He must train. He must practice draws, bursts, recovery, and control until the Well’s breath matches his own.

Until that training is real, every panic swing risks another fifteen feet of unwanted truth.

## Story use notes

1. Red and black meaning can stay a later reveal; the Well may be explained sooner because combat forced it
2. Party members may see sudden speed or a lighter looking blade before they hear the word mana
3. Dezco should sound frustrated that the gift tripped him before it saved him
4. Future journal entries can cover Yua’s explanation and his first deliberate training drills
