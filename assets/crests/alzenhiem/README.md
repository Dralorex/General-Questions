# Alzenhiem Crests

Town flag and Knoleburn family crests for Dezco’s home hamlet.

## Crest tradition

In Alzenhiem, a child receives a personal crest that embodies:

1. The name they are given
2. Their gender
3. Marks inherited from both parents

Parent crests are the fuller household signs. Child crests stay simpler, but each one is unique. Colors are chosen to match the person’s vibe, not a shared uniform.

## Notes

Child crests intentionally keep a small echo of Dolkin’s stone mark and Fulfein’s leaf mark, then center a personal symbol for the child’s own name and path.
