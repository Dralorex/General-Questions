# Kraken Boss Arena — Terrain Concept

Generated unpainted gray resin tabletop **boss arena**. Target footprint ~8" × 8" (20 cm × 20 cm) with a spacious open combat floor for a full party of 32mm miniatures. 25µm layer-friendly geometry.

Concept: water floor with ripples, 8 tentacles from edges, waves forming a bowl.
