# Why Yua Stays Hidden From the Party

*Yua story plot. Dezco knows this logic. The party does not.*
*Journal 03 records his private reasoning on the tomb road.*

## What the world already knows

A divine being was seen once. That sighting cannot be undone. Saltrock and the wider realm may still talk about it.

What they must not learn is that she walks with Dezco day to day.

## Why Dezco hides her

### The reason he tells himself first

Safety. Hunters, kings, zealots, and worse would reach for Yua if they understood she is bound to a seventeen year old Hero on the road.

That is true. It is not the whole truth.

### The reason he will not say aloud at camp

Yua does not feed on worship. She grows through hope, courage, and faith placed in mortals who still choose to stand.

If companions learn a goddess walks at Dezco’s shoulder:

1. Their hope slides off him and onto her
2. They wait for miracles instead of making them
3. They pray at the wrong altar

Then Dezco stops being the Hero she charged him to become. He becomes only the escort of a goddess.

He hides her not from shame. Her silence is part of the gift. If hope is to return to the world, it must land in mortal hands again, including his.

## What happens when he slips

On the return from the tomb road, Dezco shouted at empty air after the bridge fall. The party saw a boy cursing at nothing.

He blamed vines from Yolon’s statue. The lie held for now. They think he may have eaten something he should not have, not that he communes with the divine.

## Future pressure

This secret will strain whenever:

1. Yua nudges him in front of others (direction on the path, the two foot lift, the bridge)
2. Someone holy (Vita, Yeara) senses wrongness or grace they cannot name
3. Dezco needs party trust while acting on guidance only he receives

When the secret breaks, the hope corruption fear should drive how Yua and Dezco respond, not simple embarrassment.
