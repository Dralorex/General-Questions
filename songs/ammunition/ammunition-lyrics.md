## Verse 1  *(Am – Fmaj7 – C – G)*
```
[Am]     You asked me what I'm feeling, and I
[Fmaj7]  told you like a fool.
[C]      Handed you the pieces
[G]      that I don't show anyone new.
```
> *You held them like they mattered, said you saw me, said you knew —*
> *And for a moment I believed the safest place was next to you.*

## Verse 2  *(Am – Fmaj7 – C – G)*
```
[Am]     But three weeks later in a fight
[Fmaj7]  I never saw come through,
[C]      You threw those same words back at me,
[G]      sharpened into something new.
```
> *Every soft thing I confessed became a weapon in your hand —*
> *And I learned that being honest here is more than I can stand.*

## Pre-Chorus  *(Dm – E7 – Am)*
```
[Dm]     So am I safe here,
[E7]     or am I just loaded, waiting to go off?
[Am]     Am I your partner, or your prisoner?
[Am]     I can't tell them apart.
```

## Chorus  *(F – C – G – Am)*
```
[F]      Do you love me for who I am,
[C]      or do you dream of who I'm not?
[G]      Am I enough on my worst days,
[Am]     or just the closest that you got?

[F]      'Cause you say you understand me,
[C]      then use my truth like ammunition —
[G]      and I don't know if I'm the man you love,
[Am]     or just the man you couldn't fix.
```

## Verse 3  *(Am – Fmaj7 – C – G)*
```
[Am]     So I stopped asking.
[Fmaj7]  I stopped explaining why.
[C]      Stopped waiting for the ones
[G]      who'd never meet my eye.
```
> *Maybe this is just my life. Maybe this is all it is —*
> *A body that betrays me, in a world that lives with it.*

## Verse 4  *(Am – Fmaj7 – C – G)*
```
[Am]     I've rewritten every sentence
[Fmaj7]  twelve times in my head,
[C]      Trying to find the version
[G]      that won't end up being read
```
> *Back to me on some bad Tuesday when you're angry and you're sure —*
> *That the man you married isn't quite the man you were looking for.*

```

[Am]     I don't want to lock the doors.
```

## Performance notes

- **Tempo:** Around ♩ = 70. Play behind the beat — this song should never feel in a hurry.
- **Dynamics arc:** Verses very soft (*pp*), Pre-Chorus builds (*mp*), Chorus lands (*mf*), Bridge strips back to *pp*, Final Chorus is *f*, Outro fades to *ppp*.
- **Pedal:** Change on every chord. Hold the pedal through the silence after the bridge's E7 into the final chorus.
- **The E7 chord** in the pre-chorus and bridge is the whole thesis of the song. That G♯ in the chord *wants* to resolve to Am but every time it does, the answer is minor — never clarity, always sadness. That is intentional.
- **The final Am(add2)** — the added B rubs against the C. It's *almost* an Am, but not quite. Do not resolve to a plain Am. Let the ache stay.
