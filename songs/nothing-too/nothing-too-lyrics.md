## Verse 1  *(C – A – Fm – Dm)*

[C]      I'm a suck-up, I'm so washed up —
[A]      I don't know many things I can do.
[Fm]     I've failed and failed and failed
[Dm]     to do anything new.

[C]      I'm stuck in this eternal loop
[A]      trying to dig up something cool.

[Dm]     it turned out to be nothing too.

## Guitar / performance notes

- **Capo:** none needed as written. If the A and E chorus sits high for your voice, try capo 2 and sing down, or drop the whole shape a whole step (shapes become Bb – G – Ebm – Cm / G – D – Bbadd9 – Bb).
- **Verse feel:** Let **C → A** feel wrong on purpose — bright major after major, then **Fm** drops the floor out. Don't smooth that landing; the sadness lives in the crash.
- **Chorus feel:** **A – E** opens wider (almost hopeful), then **Cadd9 – C** collapses back home hollow. Play Cadd9 with the high D ringing (x32030) so the add9 hangs before you release to plain C (x32010).
- **Strumming:** Verses — soft downstrokes, lots of space between changes. Chorus — fuller, still not aggressive; sad, not angry.
- **Tempo:** Around ♩ = 74. If it starts to groove, you're too fast.
- **Title note:** Working title from the verse punchline. Rename anytime.
