# Sanded Down

*A guitar ballad — capo 3, fingerpicked, aching.*

The quiet counterpart to [Ammunition](../ammunition/): where that song asks
*"do you love me?"* and demands an answer, this one just names what happens
when you spend years choosing every word carefully. It doesn't accuse — it
grieves.

---

## The bones

- **Capo:** 3rd fret
- **Time:** 6/8 (or slow 4/4 — pick whichever pocket you like)
- **Feel:** fingerpicked, patient, *pp* verses building only briefly in the final chorus
- **Tempo:** roughly ♩. = 60 (6/8) or ♩ = 70 (4/4)

## Chord shapes (played relative to capo)

```
             e  B  G  D  A  E
G          : 3  0  0  0  2  3     (add9 flavor — the high open B rings)
D/F#       : 2  3  2  0  x  2     (D with F# in bass — thumb over low E)
Em7        : 0  3  0  0  2  0
Cadd9      : 3  3  0  2  3  x     (mirrors the G shape — smooth movement)
Am7        : 0  1  0  2  0  x
D          : 2  3  2  0  x  x
Em         : 0  0  0  2  2  0
Bm7        : 2  3  2  0  2  x     (easier than a full Bm barre)
```

**Anchor tip:** if your G is `320033` and your Cadd9 is `x32033`, keep your
pinky and ring finger planted on the top two strings across all four verse
chords. That drone (D and G notes, sounding as F and Bb with the capo) is
the sound of this song.

## Progressions

| Section       | Chords                                                    |
|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Verse         | G — D/F# — Em7 — Cadd9    *(descending bass: G–F#–E–C)*   |
| Pre-Chorus    | Am7 — Cadd9 — G — D                                       |
| Chorus        | Cadd9 — G — D — Em7                                       |
| Bridge        | Em — Bm7 — Cadd9 — D                                      |
| Outro         | G — D/F# — Em7 — Cadd9    *(mirrors the verse)*           |

## Picking pattern (6/8, one bar per chord)

```
Thumb — index — middle — index — ring — index
 bass    3rd     2nd      3rd     1st     3rd
```

Sustain the bass note with your thumb and let the top strings ring through
the whole chord. If you'd rather strum, use **D · D–U · U–D–U** per bar —
slow, patient, don't rush the upstrokes.

## Full song

### Verse 1
```
[G]         I choose my words the way you [D/F#] pack a fragile box —
[Em7]       slow and soft and careful, [Cadd9] never asking too much.
[G]         I laugh a little quieter [D/F#] than I used to before —
[Em7]       the version of me you married [Cadd9] doesn't quite live here anymore.
```

### Verse 2
```
[G]         I edit every sentence [D/F#] twice inside my head,
[Em7]       picking every splinter out [Cadd9] before a single word gets said.
[G]         I've learned to move around you [D/F#] like a room I've mapped by feel —
[Em7]       counting all the corners [Cadd9] I don't want you to reveal.
```

### Pre-Chorus
```
[Am7]       And I don't know when I traded [Cadd9] all my sharpness for your peace,
[G]         or when I started measuring my [D] love in what I didn't speak.
```

### Chorus
```
[Cadd9]     I'm being sanded down —
[G]         one careful word, one swallowed sound.
[D]         And the man you loved on the day you said "I do"
[Em7]       is a little less here, and a little more you.

[Cadd9]     I'm being sanded down —
[G]         smoother, softer, quieter now.
[D]         And I don't know what's left when it's over and through —
[Em7]       just a smaller man in a room made for you.
```

### Verse 3
```
[G]         I used to have opinions, [D/F#] used to laugh out loud —
[Em7]       used to know the sound of my own voice [Cadd9] over a crowd.
[G]         Now I check my reflection [D/F#] in the way you look at me,
[Em7]       and rearrange whatever [Cadd9] I don't want you to see.
```

### Pre-Chorus 2
```
[Am7]       And I don't know when "compromise" [Cadd9] started meaning "disappear,"
[G]         or when the man you fell for [D] stopped showing up around here.
```

### Bridge
```
[Em]        Maybe I should say it. [Bm7] Maybe I should stay.
[Cadd9]     Maybe I've grown gentle, [D] or maybe I've grown afraid.
[Em]        Maybe love should feel like [Bm7] resting, not rehearsing —
[Cadd9]     but I've been careful [D] so long, I don't know the difference anymore.
```

### Final Chorus  *(swell — first half strummed harder, second half back to fingerpicking)*
```
[Cadd9]     I'm being sanded down —
[G]         one careful word, one swallowed sound.
[D]         And the man you loved on the day you said "I do"
[Em7]       is a little less here, and a little more you.

[Cadd9]     So I don't want to dis-appear —
[G]         I just want to be soft and still here.
[D]         Love me rough enough to leave my edges alone —
[Em7]       or one day you'll look up and find that I'm gone.
```

### Outro  *(fingerpicked, whisper-quiet, ritardando)*
```
[G]         I choose my words the way you [D/F#] pack a fragile box —
[Em7]       slow, and soft, and careful... [Cadd9] and maybe that's the loss.
```

*Let the final Cadd9 ring. Don't strum a resolving G at the end — leave it
hanging on the Cadd9, unresolved.*

---

## Why the guitar choices matter

- **The descending bass line G → F# → E → C** in the verse is doing the
  emotional work. Every chord change, the bass note falls a step or a third
  — you literally hear the ground sinking under him. It's why the verse
  feels like slow surrender.
- **The Cadd9 → G movement** in the chorus reverses the motion — the bass
  *lifts* from C to G. That's the little bit of hope inside the resignation.
  It's why the chorus, despite saying *"I'm being sanded down,"* doesn't
  sound bitter. It sounds tender.
- **Ending on Cadd9 instead of G** is the unresolved ache. G would be "home."
  Cadd9 is "almost home." It's a song that never quite gets there — and
  that's the point.
