## Performance notes

- **Tempo:** Around ♩ = 82. Verses sit slightly behind the beat; the chorus locks in and pushes forward.
- **Dynamics arc:** Intro/Verses soft (*p*), Pre-Chorus lifts (*mp* → *mf*), Chorus opens wide (*f*), Bridge whispers (*pp*), Final Chorus is the peak (*ff*), Outro settles to *p*.
- **Chorus vowels:** Hold **TRYYY** and **eeeeverything** — those stretched vowels are the emotional center. Don't rush them.
- **Filler hook:** The **duh** line is meant to feel effortless and communal — almost like a crowd singing along without words. Keep it light even when the band is big.
- **Pedal:** Soft half-pedal in verses; full, ringing pedals on chorus chords so the hooks bloom.
- **The lift:** The pre-chorus Am is the first minor color that isn't Em — it should feel like a held breath before the chorus lands on G.
